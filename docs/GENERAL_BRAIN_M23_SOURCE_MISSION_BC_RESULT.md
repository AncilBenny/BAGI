## Decision and limitations

Stage10 gives evidence of **real learned closed-loop action selection**, but **not improved source physical skill**, mission-feature causal utility, cross-task generalization, zero-shot composition or AGI. An MLP using one egocentric front-cell cannot directly encode the full spatial surroundings or long-horizon navigation; source interaction actions 3/5 were rare; deterministic greedy action selection may lock into repeated motion. One DoorKey model seed (947) changed action paths with mission text but did not change door-opening success; seeds 941 and 953 had actor-different but mission/blind-identical action hashes on DoorKey, further limiting a mission-semantics claim. The study has only two source task families and 3 model seeds; seed/layout dependence requires guarded interpretation.

**Next independent prospective direction:** do not tune on Stage10 MONITOR712/722xxx or older Stage8/9 MONITOR. Reuse existing PyTorch/PPO/BC machinery but explicitly test fuller legitimate public 7x7 visual representation and/or class-balanced rare-action training with matched exposures and independent navigation/door/opening audits on new source TRAIN and MONITOR layouts. Observe physical goals versus the maintained source actor; pre-register altered variables, independent positive/ineffective action coverage and complete trace/hash provenance. Original M23 UnlockPickup FINAL453xxx remains sealed pending source mechanism and generic planner prerequisites.
