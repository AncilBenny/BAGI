## Frozen H-M3, controls, and decision

**H-M3:** With an otherwise unchanged M2 fit/evaluation procedure, RecurrentPPO will achieve more deterministic zero-shot size-transfer goal completions than the best of reactive/action4/frame4 PPO, across freshly initialized models and fresh held-out episodes.

- Train task: `MiniGrid-MemoryS7-v0`. Transfer task: `MiniGrid-MemoryS13Random-v0`. Independent model initialization seeds **37,53**, replacing M2 seeds 17,29. Keep four M2 matched-control arms (reactive and recurrent both receive image plus ONE ACTUALLY executed prior action, action4 receives four executed actions, frame4 receives four public image frames). Same existing PPO/RecurrentPPO architecture, rollout128, batch64, epochs2, device CPU, **65,536 native training actions per fit**, 8 fits / **524,288 total** if completed.
- Freeze NEW independent MemoryS7 evaluation seeds **96001–96006**, zero-shot MemoryS13Random transfer **97001–97006**. Do not reuse M1 seeds 92001–92006/93001–93006 or M2 seeds 94001–94006/95001–95006; none is a training seed. No held-out episode feedback enters training or model selection.
- Same five conditions as M2: deterministic before training on MemoryS7; deterministic after training on MemoryS7; deterministic size13 transfer; stochastic sampled-policy after training on MemoryS7; stochastic sampled size13 transfer. Six episodes per condition, each limited by the unchanged native 120-action episode contract. Per-episode source-agnostic success = true terminal outcome AND positive native reward; this is **navigation goal success**, not object manipulation. Reset recurrent state at episode boundaries. Seed sampled action generation per episode for reproducibility. No teacher queries.
- **Directional replication:** recurrent deterministic size13 transfer successes (pooled across both seeds, out of 12) **strictly exceed** the strongest nonrecurrent control; both recurrent training seeds must achieve ≥1/6 transfer goals. **Stronger tentative advantage:** recurrent exceeds the best control by **≥3/12**, with both recurrent model seeds ≥1/6; separately report original tentative size-transfer and same-task learning gates, not relabel them. Failure to exceed the best control, or strong model-seed instability, is negative evidence for reliable recurrent benefit. These are descriptive pilot checks with small N=12 and exploratory selection from M2, NOT statistical proof, mechanism identification, or cross-domain continual learning.
- Same-task MemoryS7 and sampled-vs-deterministic result gaps are **secondary diagnostics**, not selection metrics. Report both model seed outcomes and all four controls. M2 and M3 use different model/eval seeds; do not describe their raw success differences as a paired intervention. No Workshop/XLand manipulation skill claim.
