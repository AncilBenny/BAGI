## Registered falsifiable gates (two separately judged)
1. **Forward transfer across sizes:** pooled naive source→target **DoorKey8×8 physical full goals** across 2 seeds×24 = 48 evaluation episodes must exceed equal-target-data/updates **scratch** by **at least four**. A positive gate would be only within-family transfer, not task-independent learning.
2. **Rehearsal/retention:** pooled replay DoorKey6×6 goal count across 2 seeds×24 must be no more than **four below frozen source-only** and **at least four ABOVE naive fine-tune source retention**, while replay DoorKey8×8 can be no more than **four below naive** target performance. A positive gate would support rehearsal under this particular distribution shift, conditional on observed forgetting. If naive does NOT forget, rehearsal may fail its gain gate; do not manufacture forgetting.
- Both gates are separate. Report source-only/scratch/naive/replay per-seed and size even if one or both fail. Scripted teacher and native uniform random are diagnostics, not additional learner updates. Same-layout stochastic policy trajectories are not controlled to have identical actions. Do not infer statistical equivalence from a nonsignificant difference. On failure, preserve evidence and choose fresh registered follow-up without retuning these finals.
