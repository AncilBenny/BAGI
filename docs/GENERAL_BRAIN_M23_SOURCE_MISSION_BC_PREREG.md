## Frozen evidence and physical decision gates

Native `full_physical_goal` is the **primary outcome**: DoorKey requires effective key pickup, door opening and actual goal arrival; Fetch requires a mission-matched effective target pickup after real movement and nonadjacent initial target. Record reward-positive episodes, reward-only/nonqualifying goals, native effective events and all actual action histograms separately.

**Necessary nonvacuous action change:** for each source task and at least **two of three initialization seeds**, the mission_bc executed action-sequence SHA256 must differ from **actor_only** on at least **16/48 paired MONITOR layouts**. Additionally mission_bc and mission_blind_bc must have distinct SHA256 action sequences on at least **16/48 MONITOR layouts per task across pooled model seeds**. Count per-task/seed mismatches; a differing hash is evidence of different action sequences, *not* improvement.

**Preregistered necessary numerical source goal gate:** over 3 paired seeds x 2 tasks x 48 MONITOR layouts = **288 mission-vs-blind paired native episodes**, mission_bc must achieve at least **8 more externally audited physical full goals** than blind_bc in total; gain must be **strictly positive within both source tasks** and strictly positive in at least **two of three model seeds**, and mission_bc pooled physical-goal total must be **at least as high as repeated actor_only** source baseline (actor actor outcomes repeated 3 times only for the comparison arithmetic, not treated as independent). Report per-layout paired differences, seed- and task-stratified 20,000 bootstrap replicates (fixed RNG **231001**) and 95% uncertainty. An interval overlapping zero prohibits *reliable improvement* claims even if the numerical gate passes. If the action-change support fails, decision `INSUFFICIENT`; if support passes but numeric gate fails, decision `FAIL`. Keep the physical-science result distinct from clean software CI.

An untrained, lucky, trivial action mismatch; high offline imitation accuracy; or a reward-only Fetch termination is never a physical skill. A gate PASS would establish limited **source-distribution** learned mission-conditioned native task competence relative to this fixed blind control and source actors only; not zero-shot skill composition, broad transfer, or AGI. Negative scores may diagnose input capacity and teacher coverage, but never justify MONITOR-based tuning.
