## Hypothesis and causal estimand

Source recurrent behavior cloning sometimes physically opens DoorKey doors but fails to reach the goal; teacher labels on learner-visited states have not reliably repaired this. Before adding another loss weight, establish whether the existing *public-only source controller can itself finish from the learner's actual post-opening states*. The counterfactual is isolated by **independent real native episodes with exactly identical learned action prefixes**, one continuing the frozen learned policy and one executing a **live public-only source teacher after the first actually witnessed effective opening**. The paired triggered group is selected from a **common pre-intervention prefix**, not a post-treatment-selected set.

DAgger (Ross, Gordon & Bagnell 2011, https://arxiv.org/abs/1011.0686) concerns expert feedback on learner-induced states; Intervention Weighted Regression (IWR, https://sites.google.com/stanford.edu/iwr) motivates separately assessing genuinely executed interventions. Those works do **not** establish rescue effectiveness for this MiniGrid source actor. Stage22 is expressly designed to test that necessary precondition.
