## Evidence and reuse-first decision

P8's already-published 48-fit WorldModel/BCE comparison achieved reactive held-TRAIN-fold Brier 0.04402743 on 3,064 shifted XLand rows, versus per-action Laplace 0.04962426, but its 190-row random Workshop control lost to Laplace (0.23735051 versus 0.18776627). P8 had an extra unregistered 144-optimizer-step pre-CI smoke fit; its positive numeric gate is **procedurally provisional**, and its reactive ECE was worse than Laplace in all 12 required fold/initializer comparisons. Do not alter those numbers or rebrand them clean prospective evidence.

The E1 native TRAIN collector saved a public-visible-change label **for each executed action**, including episode-final actions, in `xland_train_labels` and `workshop_train_labels`. P8's `_shift_within_episodes` instead infers next view from the next same-episode current view, deleting the final executed action of every episode. Independently audited E1 XLand: 3,244 executed TRAIN actions / 2,019 positive public changes; P8 retained 3,064 / 1,897. The 180 excluded tails include 122 positives; all 30 early native terminations are positive tails (26 action3, 4 action4), and **all four** positive action4 events are excluded. Workshop E1 random has 200 executed TRAIN actions versus 190 shifted rows. In contrast, P8's separately isolated E2 learned DQN Workshop TRAIN data already use directly stored current/next public views for **all 2,048 actions per policy seed**, including episode tails. Thus P8 compared different label-censoring conventions across source datasets. Neither native termination nor a public change verifies a physically effective BLUE manipulation or task completion.

**Reuse:** unchanged BAGI WorldModel backbone/event head, `g2_e5_p8_public_effect` action-only/reactive/shuffled-action controls and Brier/ECE/logloss functions; existing SHA-pinned `_safe_archive(e1,"e1")` TRAIN-key whitelist and existing layout/episode split. Established BCE probability forecasting, Laplace smoothing, and group-disjoint evaluation are reused methods, not a new algorithm. The open BAGI question is whether exposure to *directly observed terminal public effects*, rather than additional samples, improves public-event prediction on held TRAIN groups and the previously censored tail subset without degrading ordinary transitions. It does **not** test causal physical effects, belief learning, navigation, skill reuse or general intelligence.

## Frozen causal contrast and compute cap

Two matched-size training contracts on each opposite TRAIN fold:

1. **SHIFTED control:** exactly P8's non-tail TRAIN rows and within-episode next-public-view labels.
2. **TAIL-SWAP treatment:** add all TRAIN-fold episode-final rows with their direct E1 TRAIN public labels; remove **exactly one distinct non-tail TRAIN row of the same executed action** for each added tail, so total TRAIN row count, per-action counts, model architecture and epoch/minibatch step budget are IDENTICAL to SHIFTED. Select removal indices by sorting SHA256 of `"P10a|dataset|fold|action|episode-id|row-index"` among TRAIN-fold non-tail candidates, independent of public-change labels and held-fold data. Prefer no state- or outcome-based matching. Abort, without substitution or rerun, if action-stratified replacement is impossible. Never remove held-fold rows or alter any held-fold labels.

Both contracts are evaluated on **exactly the same** all-action directly labeled held TRAIN episodes: XLand 3,244 rows across two disjoint folds and Workshop random 200 rows. Publish separate held subsets: non-tail; all tails; XLand early-terminal tails (defined descriptively from already registered native cap20 and length<20); and action4 tail positives, for which **n=4 across all XLand TRAIN** precludes strong inference. A native terminal flag is *not* a predictor input.

Each contract trains three original P8 matched WorldModel arms: (a) action-only (zero observation); (b) reactive public view + actual executed action; (c) shuffled-TRAIN-action, permuting TRAIN action vector only with P8 fixed RNG; all score the true held executed actions. Also compute untrained TRAIN-fold per-action Laplace, global rate and always-no-change controls for each contract. **2 E1 datasets × 2 disjoint folds × 2 model initializer seeds (17,29) × 2 contracts × 3 trained arms = at most 48 CPU supervised fits**. P8 fixed 2×192 backbone/event head, 12 epochs, Adam 0.0003, batch128, same initialization/minibatch RNG and public normalization as original runner. Only backbone+event head are updated; 0 actor/policy optimizer steps. Record actual gradient updates, all aborts, CPU time, source hashes and exact removal-index hashes. No seed retries, loss/feature tuning, calibration-temperature fitting, extra smoke fits or hyperparameter sweeps.

## Predeclared outcome, failure and promotion rules

**Primary estimand:** reactive TAIL-SWAP minus reactive SHIFTED held-tail Brier on the same held rows, pooled once per source episode/layout with equal initializer reporting; lower is better. Require at least **10% relative tail-Brier reduction on XLand**, and no more than **2% relative Brier worsening** on XLand held non-tail rows. Report fold×initializer results and source-group dispersion, not independent sample size multiplied by two seeds. Independently require no more than **2% relative Brier worsening** for Workshop random all-row and tail subsets; Workshop is a fixed-geometry negative-control domain, not a learned-policy transfer test. If a denominator is zero, support is missing, or the action-stratified swap is infeasible, declare STOP, not PASS.

For each contract and arm, publish held-all/held-tail/held-nontail Brier, logloss, 10-bin ECE, per-action positive/negative counts, group-level dispersion, and relative performance against action-only, shuffled action, and Laplace. **Even if tail Brier improves, no promotion if reactive TAIL-SWAP all-row ECE exceeds SHIFTED by >0.02 absolute in either E1 domain, or if it loses to its per-action Laplace baseline on either domain.** No tuning on any scored fold. Disclose the four action4 positives separately without claiming an effective drop, and never equate terminal game reward with physical success. Report any negative or mixed result as STOP; do not rerun altered folds/thresholds.
