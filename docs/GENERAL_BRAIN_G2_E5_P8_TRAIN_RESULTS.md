## Integrity deviation, STOP promotion and next falsifiable step

One pre-CI **unregistered XLand TRAIN-only reactive smoke fit** (held fold0, model seed17, 1,448 opposite-fold TRAIN rows) executed **144 Adam steps** before the registered run; it printed only mean prediction, no held-label Brier or gate score, and was not used to tune models. The completed 48-fits and 4,392 steps are **additional**. Twelve short synthetic determinism fits had also been executed across two local test runs. This breaks the strictly `one post-CI fit pass only` execution ideal and prohibits representing this experiment as perfectly clean. The extra smoke fit does not on its own prove score selection bias, but is material protocol contamination. The full result remains a useful controlled, *provisional* observation. No P8 model weights or online policies are promoted.
