## What this result supports and what it does not

Stage24 did not test if a separate learned recovery specialist improves native physical goals: **it correctly stopped before any specialist fine-tuning or held-out MONITOR**. It establishes only that the frozen learners' pre-opening DoorKey acquisition can be scarce and highly seed-dependent under the fixed true-action budget, especially seed947.
