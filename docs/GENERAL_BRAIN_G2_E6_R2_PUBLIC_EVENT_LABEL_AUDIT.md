## Reproducible TRAIN-only audit

For each numeric TRAIN row, read the actual executed action `a` (0 up, 1 right, 2 down, 3 left) and the **public pre-action** 5x5 view. Define a *public tile-target candidate* when the visible neighbor at `[2+dr[a],2+dc[a]]` equals SWITCH=4, KEY=7, LOCK=8 or GOAL=2. Count candidates by episode ID and size. Independently sum the frozen episode-level evaluator counts in `summary.json`. This uses neither hidden coordinates nor a learned model. Candidate counts and evaluator events are different quantities and must not be treated as interchangeable per-step labels.

**Source-verified interpretation, not a learned result:**

1. `WorkshopEnv.reset` starts with `_door_open=False` and never places the agent on the switch. `step` activates it only when the agent is on SWITCH and the door was closed. SWITCH remains a visible, traversable tile, and the 5x5 view centers the agent. Thus the **first** public action aimed at a visible SWITCH per episode is sufficient to identify that one-time activation *in this frozen environment*. Exactly 174 TRAIN episodes contain a SWITCH-target action and exactly those 174 have one evaluator activation; the remaining 34 have neither. The **533** SWITCH-target actions include **359 repeat visits** after first contact. An explicit public-history `seen_switch` control is therefore mandatory; success predicting this event alone would not establish learned causal effects, general memory, or transfer.
2. KEY disappears from the public grid once picked up. Public KEY-target actions match all **33** evaluator pickups by episode and count. This is largely a current-view/action effect, not evidence that a recurrent state representation is necessary.
3. LOCK-target actions are **37** across **12** episodes, but evaluator `lock_opened` is emitted only **5 times in 3 episodes** (size9 one episode, size11 two). In the native code `lock_opened` is emitted whenever the agent is **standing on the lock while carrying the key**, not exclusively at the first lock-state transition; two episodes emit it twice. Consequently this event name is not a clean one-time physical effect label. The legal TRAIN archive has no per-step evaluator label and cannot independently certify a public-only lock-passage target from the tile-target candidate alone.
4. All 8,192 TRAIN actions use **default geometry**. Reset-seed diversity does not create independent geometry. All 19,712 historical P0B native actions (8,192 TRAIN + 11,520 diagnostic) produced **zero goal rewards**, and the preregistered frozen history-identifiability gate remains size9 **0/24**, size11 **1/28**. These descriptive TRAIN counts cannot replace or rescue it.

## Established science, reuse and one falsifiable next hypothesis

Ni, Eysenbach & Salakhutdinov (ICML 2022, https://proceedings.mlr.press/v162/ni22a.html; official recurrent POMDP baselines https://github.com/twni2016/pomdp-baselines) support a **reused** recurrent baseline, not a novel BAGI architecture. Kapturowski et al. (ICLR 2019, https://openreview.net/forum?id=r1lyTjAqYX) show replayed recurrent states can become stale and sequence burn-in matters; no recurrent replay is justified without properly segmented episodes. Fujimoto, Meger & Precup (ICML 2019, https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/fujimoto19a.html) document offline extrapolation risk. Reuse BAGI's public recorder and PyTorch GRU, maintained SB3-Contrib RecurrentPPO (https://sb3-contrib.readthedocs.io/en/master/modules/ppo_recurrent.html), and existing MiniGrid controls rather than implementing a new planner or task-specific BLUE logic.

**Exact BAGI-specific gap:** The only apparently plentiful TRAIN switch labels are exactly explained by a trivial public-history first-contact rule; the more discriminative key-dependent lock effect has only **3 event-positive episodes**, no per-step legal target label, and no alternate TRAIN geometry. BAGI lacks independently verifiable, cross-episode/cross-geometry **specific-effect** evidence for a learned belief/effect model.

**One prospective falsifiable hypothesis (not tested):** On a **separately authorized** source with predeclared public-observation-verifiable lock-passage outcomes and independently repeated positive/negative episodes **per size and geometry**, a compact recurrent history predictor improves event-positive recall by **at least 5 absolute percentage points** over the strongest same-data, same-action/optimizer-budget reactive, persistence, action-only, finite-frame-stack, shuffled-history and **explicit public seen-switch** controls, at a fixed false-positive-rate ceiling without worse calibration, on episode-and-geometry-disjoint tests. Compare actual executed actions and reset-correct sequence histories; freeze event definitions before fitting. Do not treat a switch-contact memorizer as general causal learning.

**Hard STOP before fitting or acquisition:** No fit if a proposed effect label cannot be independently verified from legal public evidence, if each required size/geometry lacks distinct positive and negative source episodes and disjoint validation/test support, or if exact-HEAD CI/provenance is unavailable. Preserve P0B HOLD (no P1 fitting or P0B rerun), P6/P10a STOP, P9 native HARD STOP, untouched size13 and heldout expert labels. The G2-D3/D4 paired native XLand physical outcome remains direct **9/56** versus task-neutral escape **13/56**, an uncertain +4, with D4 **6,233** native actions and zero teachers; physical BLUE placement still requires initial nonadjacency, effective BLUE pickup then drop, final adjacency and native reward.
