Event counts cannot be treated as episodes or successful task completions.

The sole independently verified physical sequence was \`continue_episode\` model seed97, layout109006, action RNG51000503. The frozen policy issued 232 native actions (histogram \`[30,41,37,33,36,20,35]\`), actually picked up a key four times, actually opened the locked door once and finished in a native positive-reward terminal (\`0.6737500000000001\`). This proves **one** native physically completed DoorKey episode by the frozen learned policy under stochastic action selection, not robust command of the task.

Pooled continuation sampled physical goals: **1/120 = 0.83%**, versus continuation deterministic **0/24 = 0%**, a difference of **0.83 percentage points**. Reset128 sampled goals were **0/120**. The preregistered gate required sampled continuation ≥10%, lift ≥10 percentage points and both independent continuation policy initialization seeds with at least one goal. **All three requirements fail**, so **H-M7 FAILS**. The 0.83% evidence is descriptive and cannot establish generalization with only two model seeds and correlated repeats.

## Decision and restrictions

A weak, real signal is better than mistaking reward for manipulation. M7 suggests sparse end-to-end success despite many effective key pickups and occasional door openings; it does **not** isolate the cause. Future exploration or learning curricula must use separate preregistered **training** and independent evaluation layouts; must not learn from the M7 heldout seeds or successful 232-action trajectory; must preserve evaluator-only private effects and positive goal termination tests. Primary established alternatives include PPO entropy regularization (original PPO: https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.06347; upstream SB3 PPO: https://stable-baselines3.readthedocs.io/en/master/modules/ppo.html), and if justified later, intrinsic exploration (Burda et al., RND: https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.12894), preferably through maintained source rather than an invented learner. Neither is a demonstrated BAGI intervention here.

No cross-task forgetting resistance, task-independent continual learning, robotics or Workshop/XLand physical competence has been shown. Main integration remains on HOLD pending reviewed PR and exact current CI.
