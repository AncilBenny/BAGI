# M12 preregistration — predict executed public forward-action effects

Status: pre-execution source, NOT new measurements.

**Open BAGI gap.** M11 trained a GRU from real Memory/DoorKey public observations and actual executed actions, but its independent Brier scores were far worse than a simple action-frequency reference (0.04051 Memory, 0.05767 DoorKey). MiniGrid's native left/right actions rotate the observable view; a binary next-public-image-changed label is dominated by those simple effects. Distinguish forward action2 and interaction actions3/4/5 from turns0/1 before claiming a better effect learner. Prediction is not verified pickup, door opening, goal or policy success.

**Primary maintained sources.** Use Farama MiniGrid 3.2.0 (7x7 public partial image; agent-centered front cell (3,5), native action codes 0..6) and PyTorch's maintained nn.Linear/BCEWithLogitsLoss/Adam, NOT a custom optimizer: https://github.com/Farama-Foundation/Minigrid/blob/v3.2.0/minigrid/minigrid_env.py and https://docs.pytorch.org/docs/stable/generated/torch.nn.Linear.html . Agent input is only public pre-action image object/color/state at (3,5) and one-hot ACTUALLY executed native action; outcome is whether next PUBLIC image differs. No privileged simulator state, reward shaping, teacher or oracle.

**Frozen native acquisition.** Fresh random-native-action episodes, 32 TRAIN layouts per task Memory151101–151132 and DoorKey161101–161132, native horizon256. Reuse the SAME real episodes for all predictions; train action-only Laplace-smoothed Beta-Bernoulli rate and a simple action×front-object frequency table (fixed prior strength20), both non-gradient controls. Fit two independent 141-parameter action×public-front-tile logistic heads with PyTorch Adam, model seeds421/433, 12 epochs, batch256, lr0.01, record actual optimizer.step and training actions.

**Protected final:** Only after both fresh models fit and numeric-only checkpoints are recorded, acquire independently seeded final Memory152101–152116 and DoorKey162101–162116 (16 layouts/task). No old M9–M11 final or train-monitor layouts are reused. Score transition-weighted Brier, accuracy, observed action count and positive/negative labels for turns, forward, interactions3–5, nonturn2–5, and all. Episode steps are correlated; distinct model seeds share the same final episode seeds. Native reward does NOT prove physical success.

**Pilot gate:** both tasks have >=20 positive AND >=20 negative independent forward2 public-view change labels and BOTH logistic seeds beat the training-action-only reference's forward Brier by >=0.01 separately on BOTH tasks. Insufficient effect coverage is inconclusive. Any positive result supports only MiniGrid public prediction with an action-conditioned input; neither continual skill retention nor task-independent performance across unrelated domains follows.
