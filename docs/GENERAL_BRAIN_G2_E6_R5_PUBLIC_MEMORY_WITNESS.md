## Scientific interpretation and next training gate

A 3×3 paired alias PASS would establish only *state dependence beyond the instantaneous public view* for this particular action. It **does not** demonstrate that a GRU learns it, that histories from distinct episodes are independently supported, that action-only history cannot explain it, or transfer to unrelated tasks. `mirror_x` is a reflected *same causal topology*, not an independent causal geometry family. Repeated scripted episodes also cannot be laundered into independent trajectories.

**Next prospective training study gate:** design bounded, **actually executed** public-only TRAIN trajectories with variable starts/path histories and independently repeated positive/negative effects within both sizes and at least two genuinely non-equivalent geometry families, with disjoint episode/geometry validation.
