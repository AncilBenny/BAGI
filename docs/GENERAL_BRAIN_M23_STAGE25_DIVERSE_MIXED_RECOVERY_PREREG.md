## Motivation, source evidence, and exact causal question

Stage22 confirmed that a public expert could physically finish from **58/58 actually learner-opened doors**, but its whole-study prereg bootstrap lower bound was zero, so decision NOT_SUPPORTED. Stage23's learned, purely executed-recovery fine-tune degraded full source goals **6/240 vs 40/240** under matched real acquisition data (95%[-68,-4]), suggesting—but not proving—interference/forgetting. Stage24 prospectively protected the frozen pre-door source policy through a hand-engineered observed door-open router, but its 6,144 real acquisition actions/task/seed gave only **5 public effective DoorKey opening episodes for seed947**, below the >=8 true-opening precondition. Stage24's 338 executed suffix labels on seed947 cannot replace physical episode diversity; Stage24 MONITOR1112xxx/1122xxx was never opened, and remains sealed. Posthoc descriptive count of distinct TRAIN acquisition layout seeds with an actual open was **13,5,13** across seeds941,947,953, respectively (opening episode counts16,5,23). These were measured ONLY on old Stage24 TRAIN; none of its unseen MONITOR may be fitted or inspected.

**New two-factor hypothesis**: With a fixed larger, explicitly more layout-diverse TRAIN acquisition budget, a separately trained **mixed source-replay + genuine executed-recovery continuation GRU** will preserve ordinary source behavior better than applying pure executed-recovery fine-tuning alone, while allowing learned post-open completion. This test isolates the **mixed-rehearsal label intervention vs a capacity-/action-budget-matched source-replay continuation option** on identical new source acquisitions; it does NOT independently isolate the effect of doubling the physical acquisition budget (no matched 6,144-budget contrast in this study). Experience replay is a known approach to reduce catastrophic forgetting (Rolnick et al., NeurIPS 2019, https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2019/hash/fa7cdfad1a5aaf8370ebeda47a1ff1c3-Abstract.html). Its benefit here is strictly unproven; this is a prospective physical experiment.
