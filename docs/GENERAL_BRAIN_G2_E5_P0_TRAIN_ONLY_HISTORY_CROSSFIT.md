# G2-E5-P0 — Public-action-history predictive support audit (TRAIN-only)

**Date:** 2026-10-26. **Status:** completed, read-only retrospective diagnostic; **NO native evaluation, policy training, model fitting, new skill, or promotion**. All results below use previously published **TRAIN** traces only, not E1/E2 TEST traces. This audit does not change the G2-E1/E2/E3/E4 STOP decisions. It is not a prospective heldout success result, memory-architecture comparison, learned world model, or cross-domain weight transfer.

## Scientific question and reuse-first decision

The general BAGI objective requires public-history belief, learned action outcomes, recovery and transfer; current XLand physical and Workshop sparse-reward results do not establish those. E1 public-change prediction failed an action-frequency baseline; E2 Workshop DQN saw **zero TRAIN goal reward** and very little key/lock progression. Before another recurrent-policy/learned-effect trial, ask a cheaper question: **do already collected legal histories have repeated predictive support beyond current public view and executed action, or does adding history mostly fragment the limited data?** A history model cannot be judged on an archive with essentially no repeated history-conditioned inputs.

**Existing work reused:** published E1 and E2 native numeric traces, NumPy, simple Laplace-smoothed tabular conditional probabilities and group-disjoint cross-fitting; original POMDP/memory and upstream recurrent-PPO literature in `docs/GENERAL_AGENT_ARCHITECTURE.md`, `docs/GENERAL_BRAIN_G2_E3_LITERATURE_SYNTHESIS.md` and `docs/REUSE_INVENTORY.md`. This is **diagnostic evaluation**, not a new algorithm or external benchmark. The baseline is the **TRAIN-only action-frequency predictor**. The tabular lookup is not a learned neural policy or effect predictor suitable for rollout; it is an information-support diagnostic.

## Recomputed TRAIN-only results

Brier **lower is better**. `view+action` uses no newly appended history; `+4 actions` adds four past executed actions; `shuffled+4` replaces those past actions with within-episode shuffled commands. All scores are **cross-fitted only within the already seen TRAIN partition**. The archived JSON records 1- and 2-action results, full per-slice support and exact action-frequency baselines.

For stripped-history XLand, **two** past actions give Brier **0.056093** versus action-only **0.056489**, a **tiny 0.000396** TRAIN-crossfit difference on 421/3244 exact-key matches. This is exploratory, not a confirmed memory effect; 4 actions worsen the score, and the original public representation already contains action history. For the full 115 XLand public representation, adding true action history does not change any exact-key lookup or score: the information is already encoded. Its 193/3244 (~6%) cross-layout key coverage makes this tabular test mostly an action-prior fallback.

In Workshop, adding 4 actions greatly reduces exact-key support and worsens Brier, even relative to the current-view table, for both **unrewarded** learned DQN actors. The E1 mixed Workshop dataset scores 0.029392 (view+action) but includes **ten identical scripted episodes**; that score cannot establish independent learned-agent or new-geometry generalization. The separately reported random-only slice avoids that particular scripted duplicate. Shuffled histories often reduce support still further, showing that **fewer ambiguous exact keys after longer histories can be mere fragmentation**, not resolved belief or improved causal prediction.

## Decision and next falsifiable gate

**G2-E5-P0 diagnostic conclusion: STOP / NO MEMORY PROMOTION.** The archived TRAIN support does not justify a claim that 4-step action history improves heldout action-effect prediction. Repeated Workshop local transitions are predictable without history in this narrow geometry, while XLand cross-layout exact-key coverage is very sparse and its legal 115 features already encode history. These descriptive results do not rule out recurrent belief models on a properly informative POMDP task; a nonlinear learned model could generalize beyond exact lookup.

**Next prerequisite:** establish a **genuinely rewarded, independently learned Workshop actor** on diverse public starts before using Workshop as a learned-agent transfer domain. Reuse established PPO/RecurrentPPO or maintained exploration methods, but separately preregister a *single* small native competence/coverage gate on fresh splits; require actual positive TRAIN goal reward and external TEST completion, not intermediate event counts. For public-history effect learning, freeze fresh XLand/Workshop source-disjoint collections, a same-parameter reactive/history/shuffled-history comparison and TRAIN-only action-frequency baseline, report repeated history-key support, group-disjoint prediction Brier and task outcome separately, and STOP if support/positive-event coverage is inadequate. Never recycle prior TEST seeds, use heldout expert composition labels, infer hidden effects from unchanged views, or equate XLand native reward with genuine physical BLUE placement. Any later physical manipulation evaluation retains effective BLUE pickup → later effective BLUE drop → final RED adjacency **and** native goal reward.
