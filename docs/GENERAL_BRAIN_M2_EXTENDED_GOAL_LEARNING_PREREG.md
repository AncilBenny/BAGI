# BAGI-M2 — extended public-goal fitting and sampled-policy diagnostic

**Status:** preregistered engineering change; not a new training result until its workflow artifact is verified.

## Scientific motivation and established reuse

Completed M1 run [42935845242](https://github.com/AncilBenny/BAGI/actions/runs/42935845242), source `2165716c9a1d8781fbdd12e9e6da68c82432c3e2`, produced artifact `13817958427` (independently inspected outer ZIP SHA256 `25ea056788d9ed01998fdecfe547a2851229f2150405fc7ea76cb8371ce6c7fa`). Eight established PPO/RecurrentPPO models fitted 8192 native training actions each: 65536 TRAIN actions, 2048 intercepted optimizer.step calls, 17167 EVAL actions, 140 rewarded TRAIN goal terminations, 0/48 independent deterministic post-training MemoryS7 goals and 1/48 size13 transfer goals (frame4 seed17). These TRAIN rewards alone do NOT establish acquired held-out goal competence.

Ni et al. (ICML 2022), https://proceedings.mlr.press/v162/ni22a.html, establishes that carefully implemented recurrent model-free RL can be a strong POMDP baseline, not that BAGI learned. Reuse existing maintained Stable-Baselines3 PPO 2.9.0, SB3-Contrib RecurrentPPO 2.9.0, Farama MiniGrid 3.2.0 Memory (https://minigrid.farama.org/environments/minigrid/MemoryEnv/) and Gymnasium public-observation wrappers. SB3-Contrib's recurrent PPO evaluation contract (https://sb3-contrib.readthedocs.io/en/master/modules/ppo_recurrent.html) distinguishes deterministic from sampled actions and requires episode-start LSTM resets. No new optimizer/network or private state.

## Falsifiable exploratory hypothesis

After M1's failure and observed positive TRAIN events, two explanations require a decisive control: the 8192-interaction budget may be insufficient, or learned action sampling may produce limited successes that vanish under deterministic action selection. **H-M2**: keeping every learning algorithm, observation/action contract, model initialization seed, rollout/batch/epoch hyperparameter and task family unchanged, eightfold additional TRAIN interactions will produce some new held-out rewarded goal success; measuring deterministic versus sampled policy actions separately on matched new seeds will distinguish whether the evaluation mode is a contributor. An exploratory M2 success cannot be claimed as a direct *paired* gain over M1 on different eval seeds.

## Frozen new experiment

- Train task `MiniGrid-MemoryS7-v0`, transfer task `MiniGrid-MemoryS13Random-v0`. Four arms `reactive`, `action4`, `frame4`, `recurrent`; model initialization seeds 17 and 29. Reactive and recurrent receive the same public local image and previous *actually executed* action. Other arms use four executed-action memories or four public-image frames. No diagnostic hidden info enters a policy.
- Exactly **65536** actual TRAIN actions per fit (eight independent model fits, 524288 total if completed), PPO rollout 128, batch64, two epochs; CPU and maintained implementations. This is a matched-comparison setting, not an account-wide cap.
- Fresh, frozen MemoryS7 evaluation episodes [94001,94002,94003,94004,94005,94006]; fresh size13 zero-shot transfer episodes [95001,95002,95003,95004,95005,95006], all disjoint from model initialization seeds and M1's already viewed [92001..92006]/[93001..93006]. NEVER train/tune using test episodes.
- Five conditions per model: untrained deterministic MemoryS7, trained deterministic MemoryS7, trained deterministic MemoryS13Random, trained sampled-policy MemoryS7, trained sampled-policy MemoryS13Random. Compare same episode seeds across arms and stochastic/deterministic decoders, do NOT select a policy based on test outcomes. Seed the policy RNG for sampled evaluation; reset recurrent state at every episode.
- Rewarded goal success requires BOTH positive native reward AND native terminal goal, not a positive truncated reward. Record source commit, config digest, dependency versions, numeric-only state weight SHA-256, model parameters, actual native TRAIN and EVAL actions and resets, actual optimizer.step calls, training successful terminations, episode details, wall-clock elapsed, partial failure telemetry. At most 8 × 5 × 6 × 120 = 28800 EVAL native actions, so expected max TOTAL = 553088 native actions. Output is isolated under ignored `runs/m2_goal_learning`; M1 results/checkpoints are never overwritten.
- Negative interpretation: after more training, persistent zero deterministic and stochastic goals is evidence that more identical-data fitting did not solve the task under this design; nonzero stochastic but zero deterministic goals suggests exploration/decoder limitations, not achieved deterministic learned competence. Size13 tests are within-task geometry transfer, not task-independent continual learning; physical Workshop/XLand manipulation not evaluated. Small N (12 episodes/arm per condition) and M2 being chosen after observing M1 demand replication before strong claims.
