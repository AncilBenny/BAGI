# BAGI-M8 preregistration — matched native PPO entropy regularization pilot

**Preregistered 2026-11-11 UTC before any M8 model fit, independent episode or optimizer update.** M1–M7 reports and heldout layouts remain historical evidence, never training input. This is a focused **integration and evaluation** of a maintained PPO parameter, not a novel reinforcement-learning method.

## Primary evidence and unresolved BAGI question

M7 completed 288 new independent DoorKey evaluations of frozen M6 models: sampled continuation PPO physically completed only one key→door→rewarded-goal episode in 120 correlated sampled trajectories, versus 0/24 deterministic continuation and 0/120 sampled forced-reset controls. Effective key pickups occurred repeatedly but door openings were rare. The result fails preregistered H-M7 despite a genuine physical outcome. M6 also restored correct episode continuity (0 forced incomplete resets, max episode 256) while attaining zero independent deterministic goals. No method has demonstrated robust independent DoorKey learning, let alone task-independent continual learning.

**H-M8:** The standard maintained Stable-Baselines3 PPO *entropy loss coefficient* may increase action-distribution exploration during fresh, continuous-episode sparse-reward training, improving independently physically verified DoorKey completion compared with an otherwise identical *fresh PPO ent_coef=0.0* control. In SB3 PPO the loss is \`policy_loss + ent_coef * entropy_loss + vf_coef * value_loss\`; the upstream default \`ent_coef=0.0\` is the historical baseline. This is a **single-parameter on-policy integration ablation**, not proof that reward sparsity is the only cause.

Primary sources and maintained implementations: Schulman et al., *Proximal Policy Optimization Algorithms* (2017), https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.06347 ; Stable-Baselines3 PPO upstream code https://github.com/DLR-RM/stable-baselines3/blob/master/stable_baselines3/ppo/ppo.py ; official PPO parameter contract https://stable-baselines3.readthedocs.io/en/master/modules/ppo.html ; Farama MiniGrid DoorKey https://minigrid.farama.org/main/environments/minigrid/DoorKeyEnv/ . The project already pins SB3 2.9.0 and existing M5 public-only 243-dimensional image+mission+four ACTUALLY executed action history wrapper, M6 episode-continuity counter, M5 evaluator-only physical audit and M1 optimizer-step accounting. **Reuse** these; do not invent reward shaping, scripted key/door phases, privileged skill labels or a new RL framework. Intrinsic exploration such as RND (Burda et al., 2018, https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.12894) remains an untested possible separate study, not a modification here.

## Predeclared primary scientific pilot decision

Compare *pooled sampled* physically verified full key→door→goal rates across two seeds ×12 heldout layouts×3 repeats = **72 sampled episodes per arm**. **H-M8 PASS** requires (1) \`bonus_entropy\` physically verified sampled full goals exceed \`standard_entropy\` by at least **4/72**, (2) **each** independently trained \`bonus_entropy\` model seed has ≥1 heldout full physical goal, and (3) all four models pass training continuity/resource checks before heldout. Otherwise H-M8 **FAILS**. Include deterministic 12-layout-per-seed outcomes as an independent secondary comparison; no posthoc threshold switching to pickup counts, training reward or optimistic single seed. Results are descriptive, not hypothesis-test proof across task families; paired layouts/repeats mean denominators do not equal independent worlds.

**Alternative:** entropy regularization may preserve random action spread but fail to learn reliable causal action timing, or may impair reward exploitation; report negative results and actual physical effects. Stop reusing the 112xxx final layouts for training/tuning. A follow-up method is justified only by a separate research question and fresh split, not by cherry-picking one sampled success.
