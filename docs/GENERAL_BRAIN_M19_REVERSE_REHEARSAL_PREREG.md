## Research and reuse

Unresolved question: does old-source rehearsal preserve independently executed skill when the *order is reversed*, GoToDoor8×8 → DoorKey6×6, or was M18's catastrophic forgetting an asymmetry of learning `done=6` late? This is a controlled replication of a direction-dependent stability/plasticity effect, NOT a novel continual-learning algorithm, broad-world transfer, AGI or task-independent model. Both tasks share native MiniGrid mechanics and engineered public feature/teacher contracts. Primary references: Rolnick et al. NeurIPS2019 [Experience Replay for Continual Learning](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2019/hash/fa7cdfad1a5aaf8370ebeda47a1ff1c3-Abstract.html), official Farama [GoToDoor](https://minigrid.farama.org/main/environments/minigrid/GoToDoorEnv/) and [DoorKey](https://minigrid.farama.org/main/environments/minigrid/DoorKeyEnv/); maintained PyTorch CE/Adam BCPolicy, M18 public GoToDoor native teacher/nonvacuous actual-done auditor, M14 public DoorKey teacher, M10 physical key→door→goal auditor, M17/M18 cached-label-matched rehearsal. CLEAR also uses on-policy RL/off-policy replay beyond this BC-only study; do not claim full CLEAR replication.
