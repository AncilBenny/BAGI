## Mechanistic interpretation and next falsifiable experiment

The public-only teacher **can** physically complete the DoorKey goal from all learner-generated states observed immediately after real door openings in this sample. This makes lack of *recoverable physical states* less plausible as the limiting factor in these triggered trials. However, the learned GRU is highly seed-unstable; seed947 learned how to finish all its opened-door cases while seeds941/953 mostly failed. As a result the preregistered cross-seed robustness gate does not support a scientifically stable rescue effect. The result motivates—but does not validate—a new *fully learned* recovery policy trained on real, actually executed recovery trajectories with matched physical-action budgets and new protected training/monitor seeds.
